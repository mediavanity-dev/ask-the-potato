# 🥔 Ask the Potato

An unhinged but deeply supportive potato that cares about your mental health. Aggressively.

## 👉 [Open the live app](https://mediavanity-dev.github.io/ask-the-potato/)

https://mediavanity-dev.github.io/ask-the-potato/

## What the potato does

- **💭 Check in.** Pick your mood (great, meh, sad, anxious, angry, tired, too much, lonely) and get a kind, ridiculous response with one tiny coping idea.
- **🔮 Ask.** Ask a yes/no question. You get an answer, plus probably a reminder to drink water.
- **🔨 Worry Masher.** Type a worry and smash it into flying letters. Naming worries makes them smaller.
- **🫧 Breathe.** Box breathing (4-4-4-4) with a potato that puffs up and down for you.
- **📣 Hype me.** Fires a full-screen compliment with confetti.
- **🧘 Calm mode.** One click turns off all the chaos (it's on by default if your device prefers reduced motion). Sound can be muted too.

The potato's googly eyes follow your cursor (or your finger). You can poke the potato.

## 📱 Mobile-first

Built for phones first: a thumb-friendly bottom tab bar, large tap targets, support for notches and home bars, no iOS zoom when you tap an input, and small vibrations on supported phones. On bigger screens the layout expands.

## Important

The potato is supportive, but it is not a therapist. If you're struggling or don't feel safe, please reach out to a real person:
[findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) · US: call or text **988** · UK & IE: Samaritans **116 123**
